I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Institute of AI for Industries (IAII)](https://iaii.cas.cn/), Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the joint program of the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) and the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), where I worked at the State Key Laboratory of Processors (SKLP) under the supervision of Prof. Huaping Chen and Prof. Qi Guo. Before that, I received my bachelor's degree in Automation from Tsinghua University under the supervision of Prof. Tao Zhang.

My research explores efficient large-model inference from a full-stack perspective, spanning algorithms, systems, and hardware. I am particularly interested in **Efficient LLM Inference**, **AI Hardware Architectures**, **Algorithm-Hardware Co-design**, and **Embodied AI**. I enjoy building intelligent computing systems that connect architecture research with real-world applications.
